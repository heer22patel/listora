Real Estate CRM
01

The Real Estate Agency That Lost Leads Every Week

Challenge

An established real estate team was receiving inquiries through three property portals, an Instagram page, a Facebook group, and word-of-mouth referrals. Each source had its own notification method. Agents were missing follow-up windows because no single view existed.

Approach

We ran a discovery week mapping every touch-point from first inquiry to signed contract. We identified the four most critical handoff moments where leads were being lost and prioritized those first.

Solution

A centralized CRM pulling in all lead sources, with automatic stage assignment based on inquiry type, a 5-step follow-up sequence per lead type, and a shared team view showing every open deal in a single pipeline.

Outcome

The team now operates from one screen. No lead goes uncontacted. The agency has a clear picture of their pipeline at all times — something they never had before.

Lead Automation
02

The Broker Who Relied on Memory to Follow Up

Challenge

A top-producing broker was managing relationships entirely through individual memory and ad-hoc calendar reminders. Proposals would go out and then sit without response because no one was systematically following up.

Approach

We mapped the full client journey from first enquiry to referral. We interviewed the agent and two assistants to understand where attention was going and where it wasn't, then scored each gap by revenue impact.

Solution

A structured CRM with automated nurture sequences personalized by property type, triggered at key moments in the buying journey. Proposals now trigger follow-up chains automatically with human touchpoints built in at the right moments.

Outcome

The broker runs a reliable, repeatable business development process. The team spends time with clients, not chasing administrative tasks across three inboxes.

Lead Generation
03

The Real Estate Team Drowning in Ad Spend With No ROI

Challenge

A growing real estate team was spending heavily on paid ads but had no visibility into what was converting. Their cost per acquisition was invisible, and they had no idea which campaigns were producing closings.

Approach

We audited their full ad stack, CRM, and attribution model. Within the first week we identified three campaigns consuming 60% of budget that had never produced a single qualified lead.

Solution

A rebuilt performance ad system with proper tracking, an automated lead scoring model inside the CRM, and a live reporting dashboard showing cost per lead, cost per appointment, and cost per closing in real time.

Outcome

The team cut their ad budget by 35% and tripled qualified lead volume by reallocating to what actually worked. They now make data-driven decisions every week — not guesses.
